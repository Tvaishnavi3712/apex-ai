--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                       1. EVENT SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

At 02:14:32 CDT on 2026-04-18, Component Cooling Water Pump 1A (PUMP-CCW-1A)
auto-tripped on motor thermal overload during a routine surveillance test
(ST-RT-CCW-001 — Quarterly Pump Performance). The pump motor stalled and
locked rotor torque was indicated. Vibration monitor recorded 1.42 in/s
peak immediately prior to trip — five times the alert limit.

Initial assessment by the Shift Supervisor identified a probable bearing
seizure based on:

   (a) High vibration trend over the prior 14 days (0.18 → 0.46 in/s)
   (b) Rising bearing temperature (172 °F → 218 °F over 14 days)
   (c) Locked rotor torque indication on motor amperage trace
   (d) Audible low-frequency rumble noted by operator on walk-down

5.1  PRIMARY ROOT CAUSE.

     Lower bearing seizure caused by lubrication starvation due to
     restricted oil-feed orifice. Visual inspection (Sulzer's mobile
     metallurgy van, 2026-04-23) revealed:

     - Lower bearing Babbitt material smeared, > 0.012 in metal
       transfer in concentrated 30° arc at 6 o'clock position.
     - Oil-feed orifice (¾" diameter) plugged 65% with carbonized
       oxidation deposits.
     - Sump oil sample showed Fe = 412 ppm (limit 50), Cu = 78 ppm
       (limit 25), TAN = 4.7 (limit 1.5).
     - Last oil sample 2026-03-15 showed Fe = 38 ppm (within limit).
       Trend went BAD between March 15 and April 18 — undetected
       because monthly sampling was MISSED in April.

5.2  CONTRIBUTING CAUSES.

     5.2.1  MISSED OIL SAMPLE (April):  STP-MNT-1102 §5.3 requires
            monthly oil sampling.  Sample for April was scheduled
            for 2026-04-12 but was deferred without engineering
            disposition due to staffing constraints.  Deferral was
            NOT logged in the eAM system.  ⚠ FINDING #4

     5.2.2  DELAYED ESCALATION:  PdM Analyst forwarded the rising
            vibration trend by email rather than via the formal
            Engineering Action Database (EAD).  Component Engineer
            received email but did not formally action.  No PMAR
            issued for advancing inspection.  ⚠ FINDING #3 (re-cited)

     5.2.3  PROCEDURE AMBIGUITY:  STP-OP-2306 §5.2 (CCW Surveillance)
            does not explicitly state that CCW vibration ≥ 0.30 in/s
            triggers LCO 3.7.7 ACTION A entry — the procedure refers
            to LCO 3.4.6 (RCP) by analogy. Operations missed entry.

     5.2.4  ALARM ACKNOWLEDGEMENT WITHOUT ACTION:  RO acknowledged
            VIB-HI alarm 2026-04-15 06:43 but took no further action
            because the procedure did not require it.

5.3  CORRECTIVE ACTIONS (CAR).

     CAR-2026-0188-01  Disassemble PUMP-CCW-1A. Replace lower bearing,
                        clean oil-feed orifice, full lube oil change.
                        DUE 2026-04-25.  STATUS: COMPLETE 2026-04-24.

     CAR-2026-0188-02  Revise STP-OP-2306 §5.2 to explicitly cross-
