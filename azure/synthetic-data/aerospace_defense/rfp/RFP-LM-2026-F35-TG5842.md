## 1. INTRODUCTION

Lockheed Martin Aeronautics Company (hereinafter "Buyer") is soliciting proposals for the manufacture and delivery of Throttle Grip Assemblies in support of the F-35 Lightning II Program.

## 2. SCOPE OF WORK

### 2.1 Items Required

| CLIN | Part Number | Nomenclature | Qty | Unit |
|------|-------------|--------------|-----|------|
| 0001 | TG-5842-001 | Throttle Grip Assembly, F-35 | 200 | EA |
| 0002 | TG-5842-SP1 | Throttle Grip Spares Kit | 50 | EA |

### 2.2 Technical Requirements

- All items shall be manufactured in accordance with Drawing TG-5842-001 Revision D
- Material: Ti-6Al-4V per AMS-4911
- Surface finish: 63 Ra maximum
- Anodize: Type III, Class 2 per MIL-A-8625
- All manufacturing processes subject to NADCAP approval

### 2.3 Quality Requirements

- Supplier shall maintain AS9100D certification
- First Article Inspection (FAI) required per AS9102
- Source inspection by DCMA required
- Certificate of Conformance required for each shipment
- Material certifications required for all raw material
