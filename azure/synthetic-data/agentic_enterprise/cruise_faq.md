## Booking & Reservations

### Date Changes
Guests may modify their sail date once per booking at no charge if requested at least 30 days before embarkation. Changes within 30 days incur a $75 administration fee plus any fare difference. Date changes within 14 days of sail date are subject to availability and may not be guaranteed.

### Cancellation Policy
- **More than 90 days before sail:** Full refund minus $99 admin fee.
- **89 to 60 days before sail:** 75% refund.
- **59 to 30 days before sail:** 50% refund.
- **29 to 15 days before sail:** 25% refund.
- **14 days or less:** Non-refundable.

### Name Changes
Primary guest name changes are not permitted. Cabin-mate name changes are permitted up to 7 days before sail for $50 per change, subject to passport validation.

## Onboard Amenities

### Pool Hours
- **Main Deck Pool (Deck 11):** 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM daily.
- **Adults-Only Serenity Pool (Deck 14):** 9:00 AM – 11:00 PM daily.
- **Family Splash Zone (Deck 12):** 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM daily.
- Pools may close temporarily during inclement weather or rough seas.

### Dining
- **Main Dining Room:** Open seating at breakfast (7:00–10:00 AM), early dinner (5:30 PM), late dinner (8:00 PM). Smart-casual dress code at dinner.
- **Lido Buffet (Deck 10):** Open 6:30 AM – midnight.
- **Specialty Restaurants:** Reservation required. Cover charge $35–$55 per guest. Available 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM nightly.
- **Room Service:** 24 hours; complimentary continental breakfast, à la carte at other times.

### Entertainment
- **Headliner Theatre (Deck 6):** Two shows nightly at 7:00 PM and 9:30 PM.
- **Casino:** Open at sea only, 10:00 AM until last guest. Closed in port and within 3 nautical miles of any port.
- **Kids' Club:** 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM, ages 3–17 split into four age bands.

## Health & Safety

### Muster Drill
Mandatory for all guests before sailing. Held in your assigned muster station within 24 hours of embarkation. Failure to attend may result in disembarkation at the next port.

### Medical Center
Located on Deck 4 forward. Physician-staffed 24 hours. Standard consultation $129; specialty services billed à la carte. Travel insurance strongly recommended.

### Sea Sickness
Free Bonine tablets available at the Guest Services desk. Acupressure wristbands available in the gift shop ($14).

## Payments & Billing

### Onboard Account
A single onboard account is established at check-in. All purchases are charged to this account; final settlement occurs the morning of disembarkation. Cash, Visa, MasterCard, and American Express accepted.

### Gratuities
Auto-gratuity of $16.50 per guest per night is added to the onboard account. Guests may adjust at Guest Services.

### Currency
US Dollar is the onboard currency. Foreign currency exchange available at Guest Services with a 3% service fee.

## Shore Excursions

### Booking
Available online up to 48 hours before sail, or onboard at the Shore Excursions desk. Some popular tours sell out quickly — early booking recommended.

### Cancellation
- More than 48 hours before tour: full refund.
- 24–48 hours before tour: 50% refund.
- Less than 24 hours: no refund unless tour is cancelled by the operator.

### Independent Touring
Guests are welcome to explore independently. Coral Star Cruises is not responsible for missed sailings; please return to the ship at least 30 minutes before scheduled departure.
