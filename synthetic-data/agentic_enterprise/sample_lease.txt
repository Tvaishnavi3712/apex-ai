                COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
                ──────────────────────────

THIS COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT ("Lease") is entered into and made
effective as of January 1, 2024, by and between:

   LANDLORD:  Crescent Tower Holdings, LLC
              500 Lamar Street, Suite 2800
              Austin, Texas  78701

   TENANT:    Aurora Biotech Solutions, Inc.
              dba Aurora Bio
              Delaware corporation, qualified to do business in Texas
              EIN:  84-7194208

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE 1.  PREMISES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant approximately 18,400 rentable square
feet on the 14th floor of the Crescent Tower building located at
500 Lamar Street, Austin, Texas 78701, together with the right to use
common areas as further described herein (the "Premises").

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE 2.  TERM
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The initial term of this Lease shall commence on January 1, 2024 (the
"Commencement Date") and shall expire at 11:59 PM local time on
December 31, 2026 (the "Expiration Date"), unless sooner terminated
or extended in accordance with the provisions hereof.

Tenant shall have one (1) option to renew this Lease for an additional
term of three (3) years upon written notice delivered no later than
180 days prior to the Expiration Date.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE 3.  RENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Base rent shall be $48.00 per rentable square foot per annum, payable
in equal monthly installments of $73,600.00 on the first business day
of each month. Base rent shall escalate by three percent (3%) on each
anniversary of the Commencement Date.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE 4.  USE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The Premises shall be used solely for general office, life-sciences
research and development, and ancillary administrative purposes.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE 7.  INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Tenant shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Landlord, its
members, managers, agents, employees, and lenders from and against
any and all claims, demands, losses, damages, liabilities, costs and
expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or in
any way related to: (a) Tenant's use or occupancy of the Premises;
(b) any breach of this Lease by Tenant; (c) any negligent or willful
act or omission of Tenant or Tenant's agents, employees, contractors,
or invitees; or (d) any injury to or death of persons or damage to
property occurring in or about the Premises.

The foregoing indemnification obligation shall survive the expiration
or earlier termination of this Lease for a period of three (3) years.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE 8.  INSURANCE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with
limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence and $10,000,000 in
the aggregate, naming Landlord as an additional insured.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE 12.  DEFAULT AND REMEDIES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Failure to pay rent within ten (10) days of the due date shall
constitute an Event of Default. Upon an uncured Event of Default,
Landlord may terminate this Lease and recover possession of the
Premises, plus accelerated rent for the remainder of the term.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE 16.  GOVERNING LAW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the
laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict-of-laws
provisions.
